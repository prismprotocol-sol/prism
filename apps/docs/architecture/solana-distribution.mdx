---
title: "Why Solana carries distribution"
description: "Risk-seeking capital is abundant on Solana and scarce among institutions."
---

**Solana carries distribution.** Risk-seeking capital is abundant on Solana and
scarce among institutions, which is the opposite of the senior side. Junior and
mezzanine tranches are distributed here.

## The asymmetry

| Layer | Who buys it | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Senior | Banks, credit funds, institutions | Already on Arc's validator set |
| pCORE · mezzanine | Professional investors | Solana |
| pALPHA · junior | A professional credit investor | Solana |

The senior side wants regulated rails and a low rate. The junior side wants a high
rate and is willing to underwrite an originator to get it. These are not the same
people and they are not in the same place.

## What this does not change

The position in the waterfall is set by the pool on Arc, not by the chain a note was
sold on. A junior tranche funded from Solana is the same second-loss position with
the same attachment point as one funded anywhere else. Distribution is about
reaching the buyer, not about changing the instrument.
