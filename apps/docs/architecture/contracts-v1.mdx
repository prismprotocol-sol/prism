title: "Contract mechanics in version one"
description: "Six things, deliberately no more — and a list of what is excluded until there is revenue."

Six things, deliberately no more:

- Per-pool state and isolation, so one pool's failure cannot touch another
- Three tranche tokens
- A transfer allowlist on every token, controlling who may hold them
- The loss waterfall
- The first-loss floor and ratio caps, checked on every deposit
- Receivable records and repayment routing

## What each one is actually for

| Mechanism | What it enforces |
|---|---|
| Per-pool isolation | One pool's failure cannot reach another's receivables. |
| Three tranche tokens | The senior, mezzanine and junior positions. |
| Transfer allowlist | Who may hold a note — [eligibility](/investors/eligibility), enforced at the token level rather than on a website. |
| Loss waterfall | The order in which capital is destroyed. |
| First-loss floor and ratio caps | The pool cannot open, or drift, without the originator's deposit in place. |
| Receivable records and repayment routing | Where a repayment goes, in code rather than by instruction. |

Note what the third and fifth have in common with
[the four controls](/risk/controls): they are checks, not covenants. The pool
cannot enter an invalid state rather than being punished afterwards for having done
so.

## Deliberately excluded

Until there is revenue: the AMM, the distress auction, a senior index token, the
instant-withdraw reserve, and batched redemption.

<Warning>
  Short tenor is the substitute for liquidity in version one. These are not promised
  for any later version — they arrive if and when there is revenue to pay for them.
  See [liquidity](/investors/liquidity).
</Warning>
